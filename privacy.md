# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

## Purpose and data access
Personal Calendar Sync is operated by its owner for personal schedule management. With the owner's Google authorization, it can access Google Calendar and Google Tasks data, including event and task titles, descriptions, dates, times, completion status, and other fields returned by the authorized services. Calendar and Tasks permissions permit reading and changing data within the granted scopes. The assistant uses this access to carry out the owner's requests and configured automations.

## Processing and storage
Requests and relevant scheduling data may be processed in Codex and its connected services, including OpenAI and Google. The local gws tool communicates with Google APIs. Its OAuth credentials are stored locally using the tool's encrypted credential storage. Relevant data may also appear in chat history, local working files, and automation outputs. This policy does not claim that all data stays on the device or that service providers retain no data. Provider processing and retention are governed by the applicable account settings and service policies.

## Sharing and public pages
The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. This public information site is not intended to receive or publish calendar entries, tasks, training data, or OAuth credentials. No analytics scripts or data-entry forms are added to these pages. GitHub Pages hosting may process visitor information such as IP addresses and request logs under GitHub's own privacy policy.

## Control and revocation
The owner can revoke the application's access in Google Account security settings, stop the automations, and delete local working files or stored credentials. Revoking access prevents further authorized API access but does not itself delete existing Google Calendar entries, Google Tasks, chat history, or local copies. Those records must be managed in the respective services.

## Limited Use
Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. This commitment does not replace the policies of the platforms used to run the assistant.

## Contact
For application-specific privacy questions, contact the operator through this site's GitHub repository. Do not post private schedule information or credentials in public issues.
